The Pilot Light menu, from the flame in your menu bar.

It measures your Mac’s power draw.

On battery, Pilot Light averages your whole Mac’s drain with the lid open and with it closed, and shows the hours of battery each works out to. You can see it working.

Low Power Mode, then puts it back.

It switches to Low Power the moment the lid closes, and restores your exact battery and charger settings when it opens.

Logs out? It sleeps.

It keeps the Mac awake only while you’re logged in. Log out or switch users, and closing the lid puts the Mac to sleep as normal.

One admin prompt, tightly scoped.

The helper lets Pilot Light change only the sleep and Low Power settings, so it doesn’t need your password every time.

It knows when to stop.

Staying awake is only useful while it’s safe. With the lid closed, Pilot Light lets your Mac sleep anyway in two situations.

Pilot Light is in beta, and these cutoffs have only been tested on one Mac so far. If one doesn’t kick in when it should, please report it on GitHub.

Zipped into a bag.

If macOS reports serious heat while the lid is closed, Pilot Light lets the Mac go to sleep instead of cooking.

Running low overnight.

When the battery falls below your limit, it sleeps with charge to spare for the morning. Pick 10, 15 or 20%, or turn it off, in the menu.

Set up in a minute.

  1. Download and drag to Applications.

    It isn’t notarized yet. The first time you open it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  2. Click the flame, then Install helper.

    One admin prompt. The flame turns solid when Pilot Light is on.

  3. Close the lid.

    Screen off, Low Power on, and your agents keep running.

Download for macOS

Or build it from source

git clone https://github.com/CyrisXD/pilot-light
cd pilot-light
./build.sh
open ~/Applications/"Pilot Light.app"

Needs the Xcode command line tools. Builds a universal app for macOS 13 and later.

While Pilot Light is on, your Mac won’t idle-sleep with the lid open either. The screen still turns off.

To uninstall, choose Remove helper… from the menu, quit Pilot Light and drag it to the Bin.
